Endurance Rules (running/RowErg)
	⁃	Intensity distribution: mostly easy (Zone 1–2 / “can talk”), with 1–2 hard sessions per week. Polarized > threshold grind.
	⁃	Zones without lab: Z2 = “can speak in full sentences”; near ventilatory threshold speaking becomes harder; above threshold nearly impossible → use talk test pragmatically.
	⁃	Interval template for VO₂max: Build minutes in zone before “faster.”
	⁃	HIIT progression:
	1.	+reps/minutes →
	2.	shorter rests →
	3.	slightly faster pace/HR (same RPE). Keeps sessions “controlled hard” instead of destructive.
	⁃	RowErg technique (back-friendly): Legs → trunk → arms, return arms → trunk → legs; ~60% power from legs, handle smooth.

Strength Rules
	⁃	Sets/RIR: 3–4 sets, 8–12 (main), 10–15 (assistance), 1–2 RIR (leave 1–2 clean reps in the tank).
	⁃	Double progression (per exercise):
	⁃	If you hit the top of the rep range in all sets with 1–2 RIR ⇒ +2.5–5% load next time
	⁃	If too easy (≥3 RIR) ⇒ +1–2 reps in next set.
	⁃	Bad day (poor sleep/IBS active): −10% load or 1 set less.
Tiny steps match ACSM progression guidelines, and auto-regulation works at least as well as rigid loading.
	⁃	Fatigue brake: Stop a set before form breaks; lower intra-set fatigue (low “velocity loss”) supports strength gains without unnecessary drain.

Back/APT Safeguards
	⁃	Stay active instead of resting; exercise packages with movement are effective, passive stuff (traction/ultrasound/TENS) is not recommended. Group/education-based programs are helpful.
	⁃	Preferred patterns: Hip extension (hinge/thrusts), single-leg squats, scap control; only use ROM where neutral spine can be maintained. (Rule 3 already enforces this.)

IBS/Mucus/Cortisol – Training Traffic Light
	⁃	Green: Gut calm, sleep ≥7 h → normal plan.
	⁃	Yellow: Bloating/stress high → volume −20% or skip accessories; do Z2 instead of tempo.
	⁃	Red: Acute GI flare → only easy walk/roll (20–40 min). Why: Moderate exercise reduces IBS symptoms and protects against worsening.

Micro-Monitoring (60 sec post-session)
	⁃	session-RPE × minutes = Training load (AU); track weekly average vs. variation (monotony/strain → too monotonous = infection/overuse risk). Write quick notes: RPE, duration, HR drift, back/gut scale 0–10.

Weekly Skeleton (template; I fill it with content)
	⁃	Mon–Fri (gym slots): 2–3× strength (lower/posterior, upper/pull focus, mobility/light), 2–3× Z2 + 1× interval/threshold.
	⁃	Sat/Sun (mountains/holidays): Long Z2 trail/hike/run (with elevation) + active mobility “reset.”
	⁃	Daily: 50 pull-ups spread across the day (ladder 1–5 or 10×5).
	⁃	Matches WHO guidelines and leaves space for VO₂ stimuli without crushing you.

Technique Cues (3-word reminders)
	⁃	Hinge/thrusts: Tuck–Press–Hold (slight posterior tilt, drive hips, hold top 1–2 s).
	⁃	Rows/pull-ups: Set–Pull–Squeeze (scap set, elbows lead, end squeeze).
	⁃	RowErg: Legs–Trunk–Arms / return Arms–Trunk–Legs; handle smooth.
